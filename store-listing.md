## Full description
Kamino is a community app where you create your own corner of the internet.

**Communities** — Start a community around anything you love, or join ones
others built. Post text, photos, polls and quizzes. Follow people, join
private rooms, and keep your space yours with community leaders, strikes
and appeals.

**Role-play stories** — Build a story world with a cast of characters,
invite friends to play their parts, and let the AI storyteller narrate
the world around you. Endings you write together.

**Chats** — Direct messages and group chats with the people you meet.

**Safe by design** — Automatic safety checks plus human review. You must
be 13 or older to use Kamino. Read our house rules in the app.

Made by Kelnova Labs. Questions? info.kelnova@gmail.com

## Data safety (Google Play)
Data collected:
- Personal info: email address, name (display name), user IDs — for account functionality
- Photos and videos, messages — user content, for app functionality
- App activity: interactions, in-app search history — for app functionality and safety
- Device IDs (push token) — for notifications
Data is NOT sold, NOT shared with advertisers, NOT used for ads or tracking.
Data is encrypted in transit. Users can request deletion (in-app: Settings → Delete account).
